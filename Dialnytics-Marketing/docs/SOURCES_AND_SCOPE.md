## Deliberate differences required by the owner

- Original Dialnytics branding, copy, interface illustrations and pricing.
- No Refer & Earn, partner login or commission portal.
- No free trial; trial URL aliases lead to the clearly labelled demo request.
- Recording and Power Dialer included, not paid add-ons.
- Current unavailable features are not advertised as purchasable.
- Testimonials/customer-story videos omitted because genuine material was not supplied.
- Original focused help articles, not copied or filler replicas of the reference's entire blog.
- Comparison pages are neutral buyer checklists, not unsupported competitor price tables.
- Manual payment-preference and WhatsApp handoff, not simulated live checkout.
- Separate marketing admin/backend/database, not the dialer or the reference's private backend.
- Legal policy drafts reflect the decisions supplied, including the undecided refund policy.

## Third-party software

Runtime dependencies are listed in requirements.txt. Their maintainers' licences
apply. No font files are bundled. The UI uses the device's system fonts and original
local SVG/CSS illustrations. No external JavaScript CDN is needed at runtime.
